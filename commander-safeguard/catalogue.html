Method & attribution

Archive availability is checked against the downloaded Internet Archive manifest. Descriptive facts are summarized from the CommanderSafeguard Wiki, whose community text is available under CC BY-SA. The original archived pages and media remain the property of their respective owners.

Descriptions marked “community record” are later documentation, not claims that the exact text appeared on the historical site.
